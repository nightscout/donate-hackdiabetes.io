Questions
Is my donation tax-deductible?

The Nightscout Foundation is a US IRS recognized 501(c)(3) organization, and contributions are deductible as allowed by US tax law. Please check with your tax advisor for complete details.

Who receives my donation?

Donations are made to the Nightscout Foundation, which runs HackDiabetes. Gifts made on this page support HackDiabetes and the Foundation's open-source work.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. Stripe emails a receipt to the address you enter at checkout.

Can I donate in honor or in memory of someone?

Yes! After you donate, please contact us with the honoree or memorial name, and the name and email address of the person we should notify.

Giving on behalf of an organization?

Corporate sponsorship tiers include logo placement, team passes, and speaking slots. Custom and in-kind packages are welcome.
